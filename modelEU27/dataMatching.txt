Intermediate goods basic p. [EU 16_939_701] is now 16_738_710 in static version and 1_394_899 * 12 => 16_738_788 in dynamic one.

Before August 22, 2026, there was a significant discrepancy, with the dynamic version exceeding the static version by 2,322,399; the error stemmed from the fact that the static version reported demand (as in the EU data), while the dynamic version reported supply.

In structuralData/data_import.ipynb, cell [6] reports Naio 2022 data.
The first column, "Total" contains intermediate use of the rows' products.

16_939_701.18 is the global amount of intermediate consumption; the same column reports the intermediate consumption of sectors 1-65 intermediate goods by all the producing sectors; we use 0-63 cutting off the last sector (Services provided by extraterritorial organisations).
